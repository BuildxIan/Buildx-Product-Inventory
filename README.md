# BuildX Product Catalog

A private web app that keeps every BuildX product in one place — a searchable, photo-first catalog you can browse and manage from any device.

It replaces the old routine of product details living in notes and photos sitting in separate folders. Each product is a single record: name, price, code, category, shop, photos, variations, details and private notes.

## What you can do

**Browse**
- Scan products as photo cards — two per row on a phone, more on a larger screen.
- Pick a category from the sidebar and a shop from the top bar to narrow the list.
- Search by name, code, details, notes, shop, category or variation. Every word has to match somewhere, so `grey 60` finds the grey 60 × 60 tile.

**Manage**
- Add a product with its photos, variations and details, then edit or delete it later.
- Upload several photos per product and choose which one the gallery shows.
- Create, rename and delete categories; affected products fall back to Uncategorized.
- Create shops, and delete one only after choosing where its products should go.

**Share**
- Download any product photo at its original quality.
- Generate a 2160 × 2700 product card — logo, photo, name, price, code, category, shop and variations — ready to send to a customer.

**Everywhere**
- Light and dark theme, remembered between visits.
- Desktop, tablet and phone layouts, with no separate mobile app.

## Access

The catalog is private. You sign in with the username and password you were given, and everything sits behind that login. There is no public sign-up.
